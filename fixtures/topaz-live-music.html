Join Us For Some Summer Magic

We loved last year’s series, and your feedback was overwhelmingly positive. For 2026, we’re making the experience even better, with new comfort and hospitality upgrades, more all-ages shows, and the former adults-only performances now open to guests ages 16 and up.

Bring a blanket or a low-back lawn chair and settle in among the wildflowers, animals, and open fields of our regenerative farm on Sauvie Island. Come early to enjoy fresh-grilled farm fare, along with local craft beer, cider, wine, and non-alcoholic drinks.

Expect an intimate performance in a beautiful farm setting, just 20 minutes from downtown Portland.

These outdoor shows have limited capacity, and advance tickets are strongly recommended.
