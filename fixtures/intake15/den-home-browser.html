We are a community-centric music venue that is designed to be mutable to also host a wide variety of events and gatherings. The space features world class sound and lighting equipment within a creative experiential atmosphere and inspiring setting. The Den is a minority owned business and has been a safe space for our community since the beginning.

Make yourself at home at The Den!

Our Core Values are Community, Music, Art, and Hospitality.

Our symbol represents a deep love and respect for all who come to experience and create events at The Den.
