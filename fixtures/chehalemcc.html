Ticket Information & Policies

  • Tickets for our events can be reserved in advance online by visiting this link. Tickets can also be reserved in person at the event itself if tickets are still available. If you need any assistance purchasing your tickets you can email boxoffice@chehalemculturalcenter.org, visit our front desk, or call 503-487-6883 to reach a staff member who can help you over the phone. 

  • Purchasing a series pass allows you to reserve your seats for multiple events in a series and receive a discount on the price of each ticket.  Subscribers may receive additional benefits during the time their subscription is active.

  • Major credit cards are accepted by our online box office, over the phone, and in person. If you purchase in person, then we also accept cash in addition to credit cards. 

  • Tickets can be exchanged for other events and performances by contacting our box office. There may be a difference in cost, either higher or lower, when exchanging to a different performance or event. 

  • Tickets can be refunded by contacting our box office. Please note that it may take a few days for the amount refunded to return to your account. 

  • To learn more about accessibility and seating and at the LaJoie Theatre, please visit this page.
