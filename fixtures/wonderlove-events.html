Plan an Event at one of the best Portland Event Venues

When you host your next big celebration at WonderLove, planning becomes effortless. Flexible indoor and outdoor spaces suit any occasion, while our on-site food and drink options keep your guests happy. Furthermore, our dedicated team ensures every detail is handled from start to finish.

WonderLove is Portland’s top choice for birthdays, corporate parties, and pre- or post-wedding parties. Because we accommodate both intimate groups and large crowds, you can customize your experience in order to fit your exact vision.

Why Choose Us:

  • Customizable Event Venue Options
  • Indoor & outdoor spaces to suit any occasion
  • Full-service bar and catering options available on-site
  • A unique, eclectic vibe that’s quintessentially Portland

WonderLove is built on sound, spectacle, and community. Whether you’re curating physical goods, setting the sonic tone, or commanding the room with movement and performance, select the path below that aligns with your craft:

Makers & Market Vendors — Apply to showcase your brand, wares, or culinary creations at our curated vendor markets.

Bands, Singers & DJs — Submit your press kit and sound links to bring your set, headline an evening, or curate a live music experience.

Special Acts, Dancers & Talent — Performance artists, gogo dancers, aerialists, and unique spectacles, apply here to join our immersive live programming.

Guests & Reservations — Looking to experience the energy from the floor? Reserve your table or private space for upcoming events.

Do I need tickets for events?

Some events at WonderLove are free, while others require tickets. Ticket links and event details can be found on our Linktree. Walk-ins are welcome for free events, but ticketed events may sell out, so be sure to plan ahead!

K
L
Are minors allowed?

Yes, minors are welcome on the property every day until 10 PM unless we’re hosting a designated 21+ event. Check event details beforehand to confirm age restrictions.

K
L
Is there parking available?

Yes! Parking options include:

  • Portland Street parking
  • Under the Hawthorne bridge and toward Water Avenue
  • Bicycle parking on-site
  • G Zone

We recommend ride-sharing, biking, or public transportation for convenience during busy events.

K
L
What do I wear?

Anything that makes you comfortable—but we encourage you to bring your best self!

    • Sports fans: Feel free to arrive in full spirit gear. You might even score a perk for showing off your team pride!
    • Daytime: During the daytime Wonderlove is a family-friendly atmosphere with guests of all ages present on the property! With this in mind, we ask that you please wear appropriate clothing (nothing offensive, representing hateful ideology, or nudity is allowed). Shirts + Bottoms covering private areas are required at all times during the day.
    • Late-night shows: Be Bold, Dress Creatively, Be yourself! We encourage outfits and costumes on theme to our nightlife events, don't be afraid to dress up - we only have 1 life! We only have a few rules when it comes to the dress code at Wonderlove!
      • No flip flops or slides
      • No offensive clothing with hateful ideologies or symbols will be tolerated
      • Face must be uncovered (no face masks or face coverings)
      • Outfits must not interfere with the experience of others.
      • Flow Toys are allowed! Please be mindful of your environment while engaging with your flow toys.
  • Weather note: WonderLove is an indoor/outdoor space, so dress comfortably for the season. Coat check is available for most shows, but is not a guarantee.

K
L
Is there a designated smoking area?

Yes, on event nights, a designated smoking area is available outdoors. Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside the venue.

K
L
Are food and drinks available?

Absolutely! WonderLove offers a unique selection of cuisines from our multi-cultural food carts. Our full bar serves signature cocktails, local brews, and non-alcoholic options. No outside food or beverages are allowed on the property.



K
L
Is WonderLove accessible?

Yes! WonderLove is wheelchair accessible for the majority of public areas, and we strive to make everyone feel welcome. The Main Entrance, Food Carts, Bar, Restrooms, Main Outdoor Stage, West Turf Stage, and Factory are all ADA Accessible. The Rooftop, Lounge, and Love Island are not wheelchair accessible.

K
L
Can I host a private event here?

Absolutely! WonderLove is perfect for birthdays, corporate gatherings, or any celebration. Contact us at events@wonderlovepdx.com to reserve your space.

K
L
Do you offer refunds or exchanges on tickets?

Most large concerts or DJ events are organized by third-party promoter groups. Event tickets for these are sold exclusively through the promoter, and any questions or concerns regarding tickets must be directed to them.

For private events hosted by WonderLove: Refunds are generally not available, but exchanges may be offered on a case-by-case basis. Please refer to the specific event’s terms at the time of purchase.

Please note: Anyone found violating the venue's rules and regulations at any event will be escorted off the property, with no reentry or refunds provided.

What’s the vibe at WonderLove?

Expect a cool, laid-back atmosphere where art, music, and community come together. Whether you're here to cheer on your team, dance the night away, or just relax with friends, there’s something for everyone.

21+ Event Entry Policies

  • A government-issued ID with a photo matching the guest entering is required for any 21+ event. Passports, Military ID, or State-issued IDs are accepted if meeting the terms below. Paper temporary IDs are not accepted. All forms of ID for entry require a matching photo to the guest entering, a valid status (non-expired), and must be physically present (photos of ID are not accepted). Fake IDs will result in removal without a refund.
  • Visibly intoxicated patrons will not be permitted entry.
  • We do not allow re-entry.
  • All bags are subject to a security search upon entry. Backpacks and large totes are not allowed on site during 21+ events. All guests may receive a pat down or security scan to their person upon entry.

K
L
Prohibited Items

  • Weapons
  • Outside food or beverage (includes water bottles + drink containers)
  • Illicit Drugs, drug paraphernalia, or controlled substances (includes THC Products)
  • Backpacks or large totes
  • Cameras, Ray Ban Meta Glasses, or other professional recording devices without permission.
  • If the venue finds a guest in possession of a prohibited item, they will be denied entry or asked to leave without the possibility of a refund. The venue is not responsible for any damaged or lost items.

L
Property Conduct

  • Consent is mandatory. We require all guests to respect the personal space of others. This applies to physical touch or verbal encounters. If it is not a clear yes, then its a no!
  • Alcohol and other drinks are not allowed to leave the property.
  • Please smoke in the designated smoking area on site. Smoking of THC products on site will result in immediate removal from the property with no eligible refund.
  • Respect is essential at Wonderlove. We are proud to be an inclusive space, which means no racism, transphobia, sexism, homophobia, or other hateful ideologies will be tolerated. If found engaging in this behavior, guests will be removed immediately.
  • No Vandalism / Graffiti
  • No physical violence or harassment (verbal or physical) will be tolerated.
  • Please no moshing, shoulder rides, or dancing that invades the space of other guests.
