A Southeast Portland Original

We are a fun, friendly, neighborhood bar located in the historic Montavilla district of southeast Portland.  Established in 2006, we have a cozy atmosphere, good drinks, and good vibes.  Come enjoy our full bar and listen to great local, live music every weekend where there’s never a cover charge. We also have free Karaoke every Wednesday and Thursday night as well as pool tables, video lottery games, shuffle board, covered patio seating, daily drink specials, and much more. Stop by to fill your growler with one of our 14 beers on tap. We are open daily at noon and Happy Hour is every weekday from 4:00 – 7:00 pm.

Come on in and check us out, we just might be your local new hangout.

Located in the Historic Montavilla District
