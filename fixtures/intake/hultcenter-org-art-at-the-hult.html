  • About Us

    The Hult Center was built and paid for in 1982 by the Citizens of Eugene and stands as a testament to our community's vision and commitment to the arts. Our mission is to inspire creativity, support economic vitality, and bring people together through the power and the possibility of the arts. We are proud to be the home of four resident companies, Eugene Symphony, Eugene Ballet, Eugene Concert Choir and Ballet Fantastique.

Art at the Hult

The Hult Center for the Performing Arts is committed to making the arts accessible to our greater community, removing barriers so everyone can engage, and giving artists a meaningful platform to share and showcase their work with the public. We are privileged to host multiple exhibits within our building.

CONCOURSE GALLERY

Whil-xolik (Tell Me a Story) 
Don Bailey | @hupapaint
January 6, 2026 – January 15, 2027

“I paint our ancestors so today people will know our past to tell our stories in the future.” – Don Bailey 

Each of the portraits, narrative paintings and mixed media works in this show includes a juxtaposition of images. Central to each work are images from 19th and early 20th century photographs of Native Americans, layered with color and other images to give life and meaning to what the photographer often intended to be a static document of a “vanishing race.”  

Viewing times: 
Tuesday-Friday: noon-5 p.m.
Saturday: noon-3 p.m.

The concourse gallery will also be open the hour before Hult Center shows and during intermissions for ticketed patrons.

Future Shows in the MJCR
Thank you to those of you who have expressed interest in showing your visual art within the Hult Center. The Hult hosting curations by the Karin Clarke Gallery, the Mayor’s Art Show, and White Lotus Gallery is our first step in learning more about the process required and the support we can provide the visual arts community. We acknowledge that in this learning phase there is a burden placed on artists as we meet new challenges for our internal process and we have been intentional in working with our Gallery neighbors as they shoulder that burden and help us learn. Our intention is to use this phase, from now through to the end of May 2027, as a learning opportunity and then we will be in a position to expand access to more artists come later in 2027. If you have already submitted your interest, we thank you. If you would like to join our artist roster, please provide your information here: Hult Center Survey & Artist Roster
