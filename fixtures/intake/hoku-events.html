This international guitar sensation brings his groundbreaking stage show to the USA for the very first time! Hailed as “the future of guitar”, Misko is a globally renowned virtuoso known for his jaw-dropping technique, emotional depth, and genre-defying style.

Kivett Bednar Band & The Andy Moore Blues Band

  • Saturday, October 24, 2026
  • 7:00 PM 10:00 PM
  • HōKū Events on Madison (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

Advance tickets $15, here, doors open at 6:30pm

all ages

The Kivett Bednar Band delivers a powerful, modern take on the blues, combining deep roots, soulful musicianship, and high-energy performances. Frontman Kivett Bednar grew up in Austin, Texas, surrounded by the sounds of one of America’s great blues and roots music cities. He discovered the blues through his father’s record collection, developing an early appreciation for the guitarists, singers, and songwriters who shaped the tradition. After moving to Portland, Oregon, in 2012, Kivett became part of the Pacific Northwest blues community and developed his own distinctive voice as a guitarist, singer, and songwriter.

The Kivett Bednar Band was named Best New Act at the 2024 Muddy Awards and Best Contemporary Blues Act in 2026. The band also won the 2026 Journey to Memphis and will represent the Cascade Blues Association at the 2027 International Blues Challenge in Memphis, Tennessee.

The lineup features Oregon native and Berklee College of Music graduate Jeff Langston on bass; 2026 Muddy Award Best Drummer winner Edwin Coleman III; and Pat McDougall on organ and keys, a multi-award-winning artist and songwriter who performs with some of Portland’s most prominent acts.

Andy Moore is a Portland , Oregon based blues singer and harmonica player. 

Originally from Tennessee, Andy is a veteran performer of both Memphis’ Beale Street , Lower Broadway in Nashville, and clubs across the South. 

Andy has appeared on stage and performed with blues and roots artists such as Rattle Snake Mama, Justin Townes Earle , North Mississippi All Stars, Kivett Bednar Band, Curtis Salgado, Chris Daniels & The Kings, Mitch Kashmar, and many more.

  • Sunday, December 13, 2026

  • Google Calendar ICS

Advance tickets $20 HERE, $25 Day of Show

all ages, doors open at 1:30pm

Too Loose Cajun/Zydeco Band

If you’ve never heard French Louisiana-style dance hall music, you’re in for a real treat. And if you have, welcome back y’all!

For more than twenty years, the Portland-based Too LooseCajun/Zydeco Band has been playing their infectious brand of dance music from Southwestern Louisiana — at festivals large and small, parties, street fairs, and weddings throughout the Pacific Northwest. Take a great electric band, add two fiddles, an accordion, and a traditional rub-board, and you have an irresistible sound: happy, exciting, and exceptionally rhythmic. You won’t be able to sit still, guaranteed.

Their repertoire includes Cajun, Zydeco, and New Orleans-influenced music, with male and female vocals (and harmonies), and lyrics sung in English and Cajun French. For you dancers, there are two-steps, a few waltzes, and a surprise or two.

So come on down (and bring your dancin' shoes).

Dave Fleschner & Friends (live Blues)

  • Thursday, August 13, 2026
  • 7:00 PM 9:00 PM
  • HōKū Events on Madison (map)
  • Google Calendar ICS

Doors open at 6:30pm, all ages, $15 tickets here

A Rare Night Home: Dave Fleschner & Friends
Two Sets at Hōkū Events
Thursday, August 13th · 7:00 PM · 1125 SE Madison St, Portland, OR

Featuring Dan Gildea, Bill Athens and Edwin Coleman III

Dave Fleschner is currently on tour with blues legend Tommy Castro and the Painkillers, which makes Portland shows a rare occasion these days. On August 13th, he’s home for one night only at Hōkū Events — two sets of American roots music with an all-star lineup.

On guitar is Dave’s good friend and longtime collaborator Dan Gildea — a Portland jazz mainstay who has played with Ron Carter, Leroy Vinnegar, and Shelly Rudolph. Dan has played on a number of Dave’s records, including The Dave Fleschner Trio – Deep Cuts, At Home, and The Blues Cabaret. Two guitar masters whose very different styles lock in beautifully together.

Joining on Bass this time around is Bill Athens. Long time performing and recording collaborator with Dave, you have also heard him playing either upright or electric with Sleater-Kinney, 3 Leg Torso, Trio Subtonic, the Rose City Chamber Orchestra, the Tacoma Symphony, Seamus Blake, Dave Pietro, Bel Canto Northwest, Dan Balmer, Mel Brown, George Colligan, Nancy King, Tom Grant and many more.

Anchoring the whole thing is Edwin Coleman III, this year’s CBA Muddy Award winner for Drummer and a longtime cornerstone of Soul Vaccination. He’s the drummer Portland’s best bandleaders call first — equally at home in a Hammond jazz trio, a blues revue, or a soul band.

The setlist will move through selections from the recent vinyl release The Dave Fleschner Trio – Deep Cuts, along with material from The Blues Cabaret and Live From The Vault, plus a handful of vintage blues classics and some new tunes from Alan Hager and Dave.

Two sets. Starting at 7:00 PM. Hōkū Events on Madison · hoku-events.com
