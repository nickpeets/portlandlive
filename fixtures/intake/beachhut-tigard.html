THE TIGARD, OREGON BEACH HUT...

Aloha! Beach Hut Deli Tigard opened it's doors on Main Street in the heart of old downtown in the Summer of 2017. Locally owned and operated by John Goodhouse and his son Austin, Beach Hut Deli Tigard has become a favorite local gathering place for families to enjoy amazing gourmet sandwiches, with a large selection of great beers. In addition to Gourmet Sandwiches, we offer Salads, Soups and other delicious items. Stop in and try our famous Marley Nachos™ or our Beach Bites™!
  • Family friendly
  • Impressive selection of 16 ice cold Beers on tap, local Wine, and Liquor for a variety of old favorites and fun new cocktails.
  • Five large screen TV's. Each of our booths are setup with personal TV's.
  • A chill beachy and nostalgic vacation vibe, friendly staff, great food and music, surfboard tables, and outdoor dining.
  • New Expanded Outdoor 1800 sq ft covered patio with full stage and seating.
  • Newly Expanded space Sunset at Beach Hut, with additional seating, Full Bar, Inside Stage, Craft Cocktails


Live Music

We have live music each week at Beach Hut Deli inside our new expanded area Sunset at Beach Hut with new inside and Outside 1800 sq ft patio with stage. Space is available for room rentals. Please send us an email if interested in performing or room rentals contact    JGoodhouse@bhdtigard.com

Catering:

Beach Hut can deliver to any size event with our Beach party trays! Your choice of sandwiches, salads, chips and cookies (in any combination) can provide your next company lunch, staff meeting, fundraiser, or other group gatherings with delicious food and a fun feel!

Family and Groups:

We offer the perfect atmosphere for your family, team, or group to gather. Birthday parties, coach’s meetings, business meetings, sport events or any other reason you have to get your group to Beach Hut. Call ahead and reserve the space!

Online Ordering:

Want to skip the line? Online ordering helps you get your food faster! Simply place and purchase your order online at www.beachhutdeli.com, and we’ll have your order ready in no time! For larger orders, please order in advance as more time may be needed. You can also call us to place a phone order at 503-718-7067.

Parking:

The lot behind Beach Hut Deli is now paid parking – but it’s still free for our customers! Just come inside and scan the QR code at the counter to validate your parking. More spots, no stress, and no charge while you enjoy your meal with us!

Newsletter:

We’ve got live music, dine-outs, and other fun events coming your way! Want updates sent straight to your inbox? Sign up for our newsletter and you’ll never miss out on the good vibes at Beach Hut Deli!

Our newly expanded space next door Sunset at Beach Hut, has additional seating, full stage, craft cocktails, and is available for room rentals, for any kind of event. Email JGoodhouse@bhdtigard.com for more details.
