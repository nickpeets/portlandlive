PICKLED FISH RESTAURANT

ORDER ONLINE

BRUNCH (sat+sun 8a-1p)
LUNCH + DINNER (daily 3p-close)

Sip, savor, and eat local. Cheers!

Sample our craft cocktails while enjoying the amazing view. We also feature regional emerging artists for live music. Check out the events board in our lobby at Adrift Hotel to see who’s playing!

Open daily for dinner, and Saturday + Sunday for brunch. Please check Google for our current hours.

RESERVATIONS ARE RECOMMENDED

Call (360)-642-2344 to make restaurant reservations, or click the button below to make one faster online!

Room service orders will be charged a $5 delivery fee and an automatic gratuity of 20%.

Dine-in parties of 6 or more will be charged an automatic gratuity of 20%. Do you have a larger party of 10-16? We offer a family-style coursed menu, please email [email protected] to inquire!
