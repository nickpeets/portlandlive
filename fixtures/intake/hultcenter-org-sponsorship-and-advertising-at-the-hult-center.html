  • About Us

    The Hult Center was built and paid for in 1982 by the Citizens of Eugene and stands as a testament to our community's vision and commitment to the arts. Our mission is to inspire creativity, support economic vitality, and bring people together through the power and the possibility of the arts. We are proud to be the home of four resident companies, Eugene Symphony, Eugene Ballet, Eugene Concert Choir and Ballet Fantastique.

Why Partner with the Hult Center

The Hult Center for the Performing Arts has been a central part of Eugene’s cultural life since 1982. Each year, more than 350 events and performances bring over 200,000 patrons through our doors, from nationally touring Broadway productions to free community events and youth arts education.

Sponsors reach these engaged audiences through integrated marketing, on-site visibility and direct association with the Hult Center brand. Whether your goal is regional brand awareness, community goodwill, or connecting with specific audiences like families or young professionals, the Hult Center offers a partnership that delivers.

Signature Programs

Five programs. Five distinct audiences. Each with its own sponsorship opportunity.

Broadway in Eugene

The Hult Center’s flagship series brings nationally touring Broadway productions to the 2,447-seat Silva Concert Hall. Each season features five subscription shows plus at least two add-on performances, promoted through a comprehensive regional marketing campaign. Broadway in Eugene is the Hult Center’s highest-visibility sponsorship opportunity.

2026-27 Sponsor: Eugene Airport

2026-27 Season:

  • & Juliet — Oct. 6-11, 2026
  • Water for Elephants — Nov. 6-8, 2026
  • Operation Mincemeat: A New Musical — Feb. 3-7, 2027
  • Shucked — Apr. 2-4, 2027
  • Six — Apr. 30-May 2, 2027
  • Jersey Boys (add-on) — Jan. 8-10, 2027
  • Waitress (add-on) — Jun. 25-27, 2027

Better Together

Better Together provides families with accessible, affordable opportunities to experience live performance together. Designed to introduce younger audiences to the performing arts, this series builds the next generation of arts supporters.

2026-27 Sponsor: Eugene Pediatrics Associates

The Social Club

Live performance meets community gathering. Each event features a free lobby experience hosted by a local organization, followed by a show in the Soreng Theater on a pay-what-you-choose model. Five to ten shows are programmed per season. The series opens the arts to new audiences and positions the sponsor alongside the Hult as the community’s hub for cultural connection. The presenting sponsor is recognized across all events and offered the opportunity to host one lobby experience.

Sponsorship available — $3,000

You’re Invited

A free community event series welcoming audiences into the Hult Center through creative, interactive programming inspired by the performing arts. Five events per season. Sponsors gain direct community goodwill and brand visibility in a relaxed, high-traffic setting.

Sponsorship available — $3,000

Hult High School Music Theater Awards

Celebrating excellence in high school musical theater across Oregon, with up to 30 productions evaluated each season. Top performers advance to represent Oregon at the national Jimmy Awards in New York City. The presenting sponsor receives a speaking opportunity and the chance to present an award, a direct, personal connection with students, families and educators.

Sponsorship available — $3,000

 

Partnership Levels

 

Community Program Sponsor — $3,000-$5,000

Category exclusive per program

Become the presenting sponsor of an individual Hult Center program. Available: Hult Social Club ($3,000), You’re Invited Series ($3,000), Hult Musical Theatre Awards ($3,000).

KEY BENEFITS

  • Series naming integration for your program
  • Logo on program webpage and marketing
  • 5-12 social media inclusions per season
  • Recognition on Hult Center lobby display boards
  • Two tickets to your program’s events
  • Speaking opportunity or award presentation (Musical Theatre Awards)
  • First right of refusal to host a Social Club lobby experience (Social Club only)

Broadway in Eugene Presenting Sponsor — $15,000

Category exclusive · First right of renewal

The highest-visibility position within the Hult Center’s flagship series. Your brand is integrated into the Broadway in Eugene identity across all permitted channels.

KEY BENEFITS

  • Series naming integration
  • Logo on season marketing collateral
  • Website recognition on Broadway series page
  • Recognition on Hult Center lobby display boards
  • 12 social media inclusions per season
  • Half-page Broadway program advertising
  • Two tickets to each Broadway performance (10 total across five shows)
  • First right of renewal in future seasons

Supporting Sponsor — $1,500-$3,000 or In-Kind

Targeted support for individual programs or events. Also available for in-kind partnerships such as media, hospitality or professional services.

KEY BENEFITS

  • Website recognition
  • At least three social media mentions per season
  • Recognition on Hult Center lobby display boards
  • Creative in-kind partnership opportunities
