  • About Us

    The Hult Center was built and paid for in 1982 by the Citizens of Eugene and stands as a testament to our community's vision and commitment to the arts. Our mission is to inspire creativity, support economic vitality, and bring people together through the power and the possibility of the arts. We are proud to be the home of four resident companies, Eugene Symphony, Eugene Ballet, Eugene Concert Choir and Ballet Fantastique.
