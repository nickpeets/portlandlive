🎸TOMORROW (9/20)🎤

The Community Center for the Performing Arts is pleased to welcome back Tommy Castro & the Painkillers
to the WOW Hall!

Tomorrow night (9/20), award-winning, California-based blues and rock sensation Tommy Castro, along with his band The Painkillers -- bassist Randy McDonald, keyboardist Dave Fleschner and drummer Stewart Burr -- will perform Castro’s latest Alligator Records album; Closer To The Bone.

Castro -- the four-time Blues Music Award-winner for B.B. King Entertainer Of The Year-- is ready to take you back to how blues used to be: raw, real, and unvarnished with unique Castro originals, we well as with homages to friends and legends.

🎫 Tickets are on sale now, just visit the WOW Hall website (link in our bio).

@tommycastroband

🎤 THIS SATURDAY! (9/19) 🎹

Calling all folk fans and the folk curious! Rain or shine, enjoy the Turning Tides Music Festival at the Island Park in Springfield, OR.

From 10AM until 9PM, explore a unique array of performances from local favorites; like Laura Kemp and Larry Pattis, as well as award-winning musicians; like 2026 International Folk Music Artist of the Year Carsie Blanton and 2025 recipient Crys Matthews, and international group Seffarine.

Enjoy song circles, food, crafts, ecstatic dance, and more! Children 12 and under are free. Tickets are available at the Turning Tides website.

@turning_tides_music
