Poster Service

Let the WOW Hall distribute your poster!

The WOW Hall’s poster team will happily distribute your organization's posters all around Eugene and Springfield. Please contact our poster manager if you are interested in our services.

Non-Profit                                                       Business

One Week    60                                               One Week          60

8.5 X 11 or smaller      $63.00                         8.5 X 11 or smaller      $70

8.5 X 14                       $67.00                         8.5 X 14                       $74

11 X 17 or larger         $70.00                         11 X 17 or larger         $77

Use the contact button or call the office with questions (541) 687-2746.
