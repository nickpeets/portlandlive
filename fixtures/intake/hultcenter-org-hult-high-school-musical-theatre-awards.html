    The Hult Center was built and paid for in 1982 by the Citizens of Eugene and stands as a testament to our community's vision and commitment to the arts. Our mission is to inspire creativity, support economic vitality, and bring people together through the power and the possibility of the arts. We are proud to be the home of four resident companies, Eugene Symphony, Eugene Ballet, Eugene Concert Choir and Ballet Fantastique.

Hult High School Musical Theatre Awards

Overview
School Information

Awards
2026 Jimmy Nominees

The Hult High School Musical Theatre Awards honors exceptional achievement in high school musical theater, recognizing the talent, commitment, and creativity of students and educators across the state of Oregon. High school productions are evaluated throughout the academic year, culminating in an awards ceremony celebration at the Hult Center in Eugene. The top two performers go on to represent Oregon at the National High School Musical Theatre Awards® (NHSMTA®), also known as the Jimmy Awards® in New York City. The Jimmy Awards spotlight the nation’s top high school performers in a Broadway-style showcase, celebrating excellence in acting, singing, and dance while providing invaluable scholarship and career opportunities.

School Information

Application & Requirements

The Hult High School Musical Theatre Awards are open to any public, private, or charter high school serving grades 9-12 in the state of Oregon. Musicals must be from the Qualifying List and occur between October 1, 2026 – May 1, 2027. There is a school participation fee of $135. Scholarships are available. Schools interested in scholarships must complete both application forms.

Information Form & Requirements

Evaluators must be Oregon residents at least 1 year out of high school with a background in the performing arts. Evaluators must disclose if they have a connection to any participating high school. Evaluators must attend one evaluator training.

2026 Jimmy Nominees

Sydney Jordan is a graduating senior from Lincoln High School who is nominated for the Outstanding Performer in a Leading Role award for her role as Eurydice in Hadestown. She has a passion for storytelling, both on stage and on the page. She recently played Velma Kelly in Chicago and appeared in other productions such as The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee (Olive), Into the Woods (Baker’s Wife), Clue! On Stage (Mrs. White), and Amélie, (Georgette/Amandine). In addition to acting, Sydney also loves directing and has directed student-written one acts through her school’s Fresh Ink Festival, winning Best Director for her work on The Boy with the Cape and the Sword at Lincoln’s “Abe Awards.” This fall, Sydney will attend Ithaca College to pursue a BFA in Acting, where she looks forward to continuing to grow as a performer, collaborator, director, and artist. She would like to thank her parents for the opportunities and support they have provided to follow her passions.

Caden Tubbs is a graduate from Hood River Valley High School, in Hood River, Oregon. He has participated in theatre arts, singing, and dancing for many years, and he doesn’t plan on stopping! Caden is committed to Molloy University’s CAP21 Musical Theatre Conservatory in New York. No matter where Caden ends up in life, he will forever be grateful for the opportunities given to him by his parents, sister, girlfriend, teachers, directors, friends, as well as many, MANY others! He would love to give a special shoutout to Tay Camille Lynne, Rachel Harry, Jennifer Graves, and Chase Del Rey for giving him countless chances to improve and grow as a performer. Caden hopes to see many of you on the big stages in his future!
