T3 EVENTS FAQ

How much is tonight’s show?

Turn! Turn! Turn! promotes a Do-It-Yourself esthetic. We ask that performers build their own bills, set the price for the event, and decide on the money split. We believe this approach helps promote a music-community mindset, rather than a venue/performer divide. Generally, most shows cost $10-$20, while no one will be turned away due to lack of funds.

How much do the bands make at Turn!?

Performers take home the bulk of the door money. The more you give at the door, the more the bands get! After paying the door person, the rest of the proceeds go to the performers.

Can I pay with a credit card/phone/subdermal micro-chip?

Admission to the event is cash only at the doors. However, you can purchase advance tickets at the link for each event. This makes it easy for us to pass the money directly to the performers at the end of the event. There are ATMs within walking distance of the venue.

What about my beer and pizza?

The bar accepts all payment types for food and drink. This is how the venue covers ALL of its operating expenses. The more regularly you eat and drink at Turn!, the more likely we will still be here doing this for you next year!

With love and gratitude, we hope this FAQ has been illuminating.

Thanks for reading!
