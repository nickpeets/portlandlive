THE DOSE

After last year's SOLD OUT success THE DOSE returns to THE SIREN THEATER in PORTLAND OR for another mind-bending journey of experimentation, laughter, and healing. Set in a vibrant and surreal venue, THE DOSE is a fully immersive psychedelic experience featuring a carefully curated line-up of some of the best comedians in the country (seen on HBO, Comedy Central, Late Night, Netflix, Don’t Tell, and more!).
At THE DOSE we facilitate the perfect set and setting for both the comedians and the audience to find the funny within their expanding minds.
Our Master of Ceremonies and creator of THE DOSE is Steven Gillespie (CONAN, AMAZON PRIME), a nationally touring stand-up comedian and a psychedelic connoisseur.
THE DOSE is more than a comedy show, it’s a multi-media event featuring our very own DJ and he will play before, during, and sometimes even after the show! Don't miss the pre-show DJ set (starts at 6:30pm doors) featuring music, visuals, vendors and flow performers...its becoming more and more popular!​
Come take a trip with us as we transcend the ordinary, dance on the edges of perception, and explore the healing properties of our boundless imaginations. And also to just laugh and be silly :)

GENERAL SHOW INFORMATION

The Siren is all ages but not all show content is. Please use your own discretion when bringing a younger audience member. Most shows are  appropriate for those 18+.

Please refrain from taking pictures or video during performances, unless you have been asked to do so.  It's distracting to the performers and we just want to be in the moment with you!

The Siren is a NO HECKLING ZONE. Please don't talk to any performer unless they address you first.  If you are inebriated, heckling, or causing any disruption, you’ll be asked to leave the theater and will not receive a refund. Thanks!

Advanced online ticketing ends 1-2 hours before start of show, then tickets are sold at the box office beginning when doors open for shows that are not sold out online. Advanced tickets are typically cheaper than tickets purchased at the door.

Shows that are labeled "Visiting Productions"  are not produced by The Siren Theater. These shows are from outside performers and producers who rent The Siren for their production. They have full control over ticketing, show content and production value. Questions about their shows should be directed to them. 

Tickets are nontransferable and nonrefundable.

Doors open 30-45 minutes before start of show.
