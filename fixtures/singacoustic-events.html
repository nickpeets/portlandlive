Rooster Blackspur: Folk, Roots & Americana

Raised in the Alaskan wilderness and shaped by years spent in the high desert of New Mexico, Rooster Blackspur is an award-winning singer-songwriter whose music blends folk-roots, country rock and soulful Americana. Her commanding voice moves effortlessly between angelic clarity and road-worn grit, carrying songs filled with resilience, loss, reinvention and the untamed spirit of the American West.

Before launching her solo career, Blackspur performed as part of Moody Little Sister from 2010 to 2019. She was named Singer-Songwriter of the Year at the 2021 New Mexico Music Awards and has appeared at the Sisters Folk Festival, Britt Festival, Aladdin Theater, Mile of Music and other venues and festivals across the country.

Now based in Nashville, Blackspur recorded Nashville (The Cinderella Sessions) live at the historic Cinderella Sound Studio with an accomplished group of Nashville musicians, including Charlie McCoy, Bill Cooley and Steve Hinson. Her performances combine powerful original songs, candid storytelling and a magnetic stage presence that turns a concert into a deeply human experience.

Bryan Bowers: Master of the Autoharp

For over six decades, Bryan Bowers has been to the autoharp what Earl Scruggs was to the fivestring banjo. He presents instrumental virtuosity combined with warmth, eloquence, expression, and professionalism.

Born in Yorktown, Virginia, Bryan Bowers was raised in New Bohemia. Bowers discovered music when he took up the guitar.“The roots of the music had gone real deep in me. Music was real fulfilling; unlike anything I had ever done before.”

It wasn’t long before Bowers encountered the autoharp. “I ran into a guy that played several instruments and could get the harp in good tune. He played without any fingerpicks, just with his fingernails. He had a real sprightly style on it. It was the first time I’d heard someone play it in good tune and play it well. It opened my eyes and my ears. I went out and got one the next day.”

 

This is an All Ages Event.

The Art of the Protest Song

Three distinctive voices come together for The Art of Protest, an intimate evening of songs, stories, and music that refuses to remain silent. Performing in the round, Bill Valenti, Darka Dusty, and Patrick Tovatt will trade songs and perspectives, exploring the power of music to question, challenge, unite, and inspire change. Join us at Singacoustic on Thursday, October 22 at 7:00 PM for a timely night of courage, conscience, and connection.

Bill Valenti, is a Portland, Oregon songwriter who believes in the power of the protest song to inspire political activism.  He launched the Art of the Protest Song project in 2012, and has produced 30 performances around the country, focused on political, social, economic and environmental issues.  Bill’s topical songwriting is influenced by the massive popular uprisings in the US, Europe and Asia he witnessed in the 1960s and 70s.  The pen is mightier than the sword, and a song is mightier still.

Darka Dusty, Stebivka is a first-generation Ukrainian-American and a lifelong musician and singer. Growing up Ukrainian in America means Darka’s been wired for political resistance and fighting for the truth. Performing with Darka will be her partner-in-time Miri Stebivka. Together, they’ve been on a long, creative journey as musicians and photographers, having just celebrated 30 years together.

Patrick Tovatt, after a fifty-year career in the theatre, television and film, as actor, director and writer, retired and left NYC for Oregon to hang with his son Daniel. Now he scratches the creative itch by writing poems, essays and songs. He and his dear Valerie float on the Columbia River, feed the ducks and grow tomatoes.

Nathan Stanley: A Tribute to Dr. Ralph Stanley

Nathan Stanley was born into one of bluegrass music’s most celebrated families. As the grandson of the legendary Dr. Ralph Stanley, Nathan grew up performing alongside his grandfather and the Clinch Mountain Boys, absorbing the traditional mountain sound that shaped generations of American music.

A Dove Award winner and Grammy nominee, Nathan has built his own career blending bluegrass, country and gospel while preserving the songs and spirit of his family’s musical heritage. In this special performance, he honors Dr. Ralph Stanley through the timeless music, stories and unmistakable mountain soul that made his grandfather an American icon.

All Over the Road: John Silliman Dodge & Michael Henchman

John Silliman Dodge

John Silliman Dodge has spent more than 50 years playing guitar, writing songs and composing instrumental music. A former ATCO Records recording artist, John toured extensively with Cooper Dodge, sharing bills with James Taylor, Jerry Garcia, Jimmy Buffett, Joe Cocker, Doc Watson and other celebrated performers.

His musical range stretches from folk, rock and jazz to classical and flamenco. A student of guitar masters Tommy Emmanuel, Richard Thompson and Pierre Bensusan, John describes his performances as “360 degrees of songwriting and guitar”, music that can travel from Bach to Hendrix without losing its way.

https://johndodgemusic.com

Michael Henchman

Michael Henchman creates what he calls “Road Dust Folk”: thoughtful, imagery-rich songs shaped by distant roads, wide landscapes and the search for connection. His years living throughout the United States and Europe—including three decades in Alaska—give his writing a cinematic sense of place and a deep understanding of time, distance and belonging.

His 2025 album, If the Sky Fell, blends contemporary folk, Americana, roots, country and touches of jazz. The album reached No. 12 on the Folk Alliance International Folk Chart. Michael is also an accomplished guitarist, bassist and vocalist whose songwriting has earned recognition in numerous national competitions.

https://michaelhenchman.com

 

This is a free show.

Brian Gore: Acoustic Fingerstyle Journeys

“Guitar Poet” Brian Gore Brings His Harp Guitar, Foot Drums, and Emotive Songwriting to Portland’s Sing Acoustic on December 5th

The founder of International Guitar Night will perform an intimate, one-night-only set featuring his powerful new release, “The Pathless Chosen.  

Known as the “Guitar Poet,” acclaimed singer-songwriter and fingerstyle guitarist Brian Gore will bring his deeply personal, expansive acoustic soundscapes to Portland’s premier listening room, Sing Acoustic, for a captivating evening of music and storytelling on Saturday, December 5, 2026.

Playing in service of song over showmanship, Gore’s live performances pair raw, emotive vocals with the deep, resonant frequencies of a rare harp guitar, playing perfectly suited for Sing Acoustic’s dedicated listening-room environment.

The performance will feature live renditions of his acclaimed new single, “The Pathless Chosen.” The song serves as a musical wake-up call—an invitation for listeners to open their eyes and step into the unknown as pioneers of their own existence. 

“There is something magical about stepping onto a stage where the audience is fully dialed into the storytelling and the subtle nuances of the instrument,” says Gore. “The harp guitar naturally creates this massive, open room to breathe, and Sing Acoustic is the absolute ideal space to share the vulnerability and adventure behind ‘The Pathless Chosen.'” 

$15 Advance. $20 at the door.
