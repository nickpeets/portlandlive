What the FAQ?

Answers to common questions

How do I know if a show is All Ages or 21+?

Atlantis Lounge is All Ages until 9:00 PM, at that time any minors will be asked to leave the space. This is noted in the event description for all shows.

Do you have a parking lot?

We do not, but there is ample street parking on Mississippi Ave and neighboring streets.

Can you accommodate ADA requests?

Yes! Please email atlantisbookingpdx@gmail.com with specific accommodation requests, your order number (you will receive this via email from Etix) and what kind of accommodation you need.

I think I left something behind at a show, do you have a Lost and Found?

Yes! Please give us a call at (503) 288-3231 and a employee can check our Lost and Found for your item.

How do I book a show at your venue?

Please send an email to atlantisbookingpdx@gmail.com or use the booking submission form.

How do I book a private event?

Please use the private event booking form. We host private events in our lounge space and bar space.

The price for tickets looks odd, what's up with that?

For online ticket purchases, there is a 10% ticket fee (minimum of $1.50) per show. The price you see on ourwebsite is the total cost, including the ticket fee.
